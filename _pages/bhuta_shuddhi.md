Bhuta Shuddhi is a whole system of yoga meaning "purification of the elements," intended to establish wellbeing of body and mind.

The basis of all creation, including the physical body, is the five elements: earth, water, wind, fire and space. Purifying these five elements within the human system establishes wellbeing of body and mind, and shapes the body into a stepping stone toward ultimate wellbeing.

This esoteric yogic science otherwise requires intense sadhana (spiritual practice). Isha's Bhuta Shuddhi processes are offered so that anyone can benefit.

## Benefits

- Keeps the system in harmony and balance
- Prepares the system to handle powerful states of energy
- Enhances the capabilities of the physical body, mind and energy system
- Creates the basis to gain complete mastery over the human system

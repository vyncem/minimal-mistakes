Isha Hatha Yoga is an opportunity to learn classical practices maintained in their full purity and vibrancy. These are offered not merely as physical exercises, but as a holistic process to align the body, mind and energy system and bring a deeper sense of wellbeing.

## Yogasanas

{% include video id="4ZdcGKUQufU" provider="youtube" %}

The word *asana* literally means posture. Isha’s Yogasanas are a subtle process for changing energy in the system to straighten out physical and mental imbalances. The program is offered as a set of 36 powerful postures that require no special agility or previous experience.

- A gentle path to improved health, balance and focus
- Supports the evolution of body and mind toward a higher possibility
- Stabilizes the body, mind and energy system
- Helps decelerate the ageing process

Yogasanas are not simply physical movements; they are designed to enable the human system to sustain higher dimensions of energy and improve the experience of deeper practices such as kriyas and meditation.

## Surya Kriya

{% include video id="_wq-OiPk-pU" provider="youtube" %}

“Surya” means sun and “kriya” means inner energy process. Surya Kriya is a powerful yogic practice that activates the solar plexus and raises *samat prana*, the solar heat in the system.

- Develops mental clarity and focus
- Remedies weak constitutions
- Boosts vigor and vitality
- Balances hormonal levels in the body
- Prepares one for deeper states of meditation

Surya Kriya balances the left and right energy channels, creating stability in the body and stillness in the mind.

## Angamardana

{% include video id="9aAzFTQOJJU" provider="youtube" %}

Angamardana means gaining mastery over the limbs, organs and other parts of the body. It is a yoga-rooted fitness system that revitalizes the body and prepares it for the full depth of Hatha Yoga.

- Strengthens the spine, skeletal system and muscular system
- Builds physical strength, fitness and tenacity
- Takes years off the body, bringing lightness and freedom
- Prepares the body for Hatha Yoga

These practices are accessible to everyone and require no equipment, making them practical for daily life and travel.
